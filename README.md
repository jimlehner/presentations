# Presentations

This repository contains the Python code and supporting files associated with in person and digital presentations given on behalf of Broken Quality Press, The Broken Quality Initiative, and Lehner Process Solutions.

Have a group that you think I should speak with? Contact me (Jim Lehner) at QualityIsBroken@gmail.com or through the [contact page](https://jimlehner.github.io/contact/).

![](figures/is_predictable_always_capable.png)

## List of presentations

- [The Challenge of manufacturing](#the-challenge-of-manufacturing)
- [The Definition of Quality](#the-definition-of-quality)
- [Description, Modeling, and Characterization](#description-modeling-and-characterization)

### The Challenge of Manufacturing
This presentation explores the fundamental challenge of manufacturing and presents the theory, tools, and techniques that make overcoming that challenge possible. 

### The Definition of Quality
This presentation presents the case for using the operational definition of quality established by Dr. Genichi Taguchi: on-target with minimum variance.  

### Description, Modeling, and Characterization
This presentation compares and contrasts the benefits of descriptive analytical techniques, modeling techniques, and characterization of process data.
